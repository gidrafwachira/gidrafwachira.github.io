Digital portfolio leadership across 10+ countries Competencies: strategy, governance, people Executive summary I directed 4 multi-country digital programmes across health, agriculture and climate, covering 10+ East African, West African and South-East Asian countries, with governance and performance systems built for each context. Challenge Each programme sat in a different regulatory, cultural and donor environment, in remote-first delivery, and needed rigorous governance alongside room to adapt. Approach • Designed governance, reporting and performance management from the start of each cycle • Directed multicultural teams, budgets and resources, refining delivery with MEL data and a lessons-learned matrix • Evaluated programmes and wrote quarterly donor reports and annual portfolio updates Results • 95% of programmes delivered on time and within budget • 250,000+ smallholders reached, with 73%-122% income uplift • Delivery bottlenecks cut 30% Takeaway At this scale you need structure that holds under pressure and enough flexibility to adapt. Climate strategy, finance and grantmaking Competencies: strategy, financial stewardship, compliance Executive summary I designed adaptation and climate-smart agriculture strategy and moved capital to the partners who could deliver it, across 7+ markets. Challenge Strategy only counts when it becomes funded, accountable action, and small community organisations need a process they can actually complete. Approach • Ran every stage: call, due diligence, award, monitoring and reporting • Funded 9 INGOs and 16 CBOs and worked with national and local governments • Published an Early Warning Systems training course for community use Results • $3M+ deployed to 25+ organisations • 97% compliance and audit readiness • Processing time cut 30% • Weather index insurance and advisory services reached 100,000+ smallholders, raising farm output 27% Takeaway Simple, well-documented grant processes widen who can get funded while strengthening compliance.
